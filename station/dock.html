How this dock works

Add
Appends to the END of the queue and saves instantly. The on-air item is never affected; new things play once everything above them has finished. Duplicate links/files are rejected.
Order
Plays top to bottom. The NEXT marker shows what loads after the on-air item. Drag rows to reorder (saved instantly), or use the row menu.
Row menu …
Move, toggle dormant (dimmed = never plays), play next, or remove. The on-air row cannot be removed or double-clicked.
Double-click a row
Play now: the row is moved to the head of the queue and the on-air item is ended. Use with care — it cuts what is playing.
Cache dot
filled = full local copy downloaded; a cut here works with no internet. pulsing = downloading right now. hollow = streams live from YouTube (needs the internet; can still die mid-play).
Save
A selected box entry is overwritten; with nothing selected a new saved playlist is created. Either way your WORKING queue is already saved instantly on every edit — Save only versions it.
Load
Replaces the queue from a saved playlist. It swaps at the next cut: the on-air item keeps playing to its natural end.
Delete
Two presses within 3s confirm.
reshuffle checkbox
End-of-roll behavior: when the queue finishes, shuffle it and restart from the top automatically.
Shuffle rest
Shuffles everything except the on-air item and saves. NEXT recalculates.
Prev / Skip
Skip ends the on-air item now and starts NEXT (~5s deck swap). Prev queues the previous item and ends the current. Skip is debounced to once per 4s.
Dormant rows
Kept in the list but never air. Use for content you rotate in later.
