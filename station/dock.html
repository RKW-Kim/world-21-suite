How this dock works

Add
Pastes become items at the END of the queue and save instantly. A YouTube playlist link (one with list= in it) is detected automatically: it imports as a parked group below the queue, in the playlist's own order — nothing airs until you move items in. Imports only read titles, nothing downloads.
Order
Plays top to bottom; the NEXT marker shows what really airs after the on-air item (parked rows are skipped). Drag rows to reorder (drop ≤on≤ a row to insert there, drop on empty space to append; a 2s re-render pause no longer breaks drags), or use the row menu ⋯.
Row menu ⋯
Move, park/wake (Available = parked, never airs, dimmed), play next, tag as Video/Music (which library tab it lives in), normalize audio (file items: in-place EBU-R128 pass; YouTube items: a fresh spool through the loudness pass), or remove. Items that YouTube renamed carry a yellow meta? badge: approve (keep the new title) or discard (delete the row). On-air rows are protected.
Double-click a row
Play now: the row moves under the cursor and the on-air item is ended. Use with care — it cuts what is playing.
Groups
Parked playlists live down here, folded by default, under the tab their rows are tagged for. Click a header to fold/unfold. Drag the header straight into the queue to play the whole list there; drag single rows out one at a time. Header buttons: Play (queue at the cursor + cut now), shuf (randomize within the group), orig (restore the playlist's import order).
Dropping rules
Drop a row on the queue to air it there; on a group to park it inside that playlist; on the Available header to park it outside any list.
Cache dot
filled = full local copy downloaded; cuts here work with no internet. pulsing = downloading now. Hollow = live from YouTube.
Save version / Load / Delete
The queue saves itself on every edit — Save version only snapshots the current queue under a name as a restore point. Load replaces the queue at the next cut. Delete needs two presses within 3s.
Shuffle (ACTIVE header)
Shuffle randomizes the waiting rows and saves; the on-air item keeps its slot. The shuf at end checkbox reshuffles automatically when the roll finishes.
Prev / Skip
Skip ends the on-air item now and starts NEXT (~5s deck swap, debounced 4s). Prev queues the item before the on-air one.
Replay / Pause
File decks only. Replay restarts from 0:00; Pause freezes (Resume unfreezes). A live YouTube stream can't be paused.
ON-AIR VOL
The deck's dB mix, remembered across cuts and restarts. 0 dB = full. Parked decks are muted and paused automatically after every cut.
If the dock says…
PANEL UNREACHABLE: the dock lost the panel — press Retry now. DAEMON STALLED: the panel answers but the playout loop hasn't ticked in over 12s — Restart daemon (two presses).
