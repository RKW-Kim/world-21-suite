How to choose — innovation vs proven, mapped

The proven spine: a glass card that says SUBSCRIBE or FOLLOW is the industry-standard pattern — every big stream runs some version of it. What changed here is the material truth: real edge refraction and specular rims ported from the best open-source iOS 26 recreations, not a blurred rectangle pretending to be glass. That alone separates you from the parody tier.

The innovation layer: D3's money moments — a coin engraved with your own smile face, an M-PESA tile that Kenyan viewers instantly recognise, amount count-ups with tabular numerals. These are rare, physical and local: exactly the traits that make a "super thanks" feel like an event instead of a nag. Rarity is the point — if spectacle fires every five minutes it becomes noise; keep it for paid interactions only.

The recommendation: D1 CLEAR as the daily driver (it is the same personality as your bare watermark — light, quiet, elegant), OBSIDIAN when the content runs dark, D3 SPECTACLE exclusively when money lands.
