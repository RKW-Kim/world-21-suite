THE ANSWER YOU ASKED FOR

Handles in the watermark, or a separate bottom-right animation? Both — they're different jobs.

You said the quiet part out loud: "there is a line of innovation and doing what is proven to work — how about we do them all." Agreed. That's why there are seven variants instead of one. But here's the division of labour that makes them compose instead of compete:

THE WATERMARK = IDENTITY

Always on, never moves, never changes size mid-stream. Its only job is "whose stream is this?" — recognition at a glance, not reading. It answers with the face mark and stays out of the content's way. A watermark that animates too much stops being furniture and starts being a pop-up.

YOUR MORPH IDEA (V2) = THE PROVEN PATTERN

The writing disappears and returns as subscribe / follow / #TradeSmile / @handles — this is exactly how broadcast idents and YouTube subscribe stingers behave, so it's innovation built on a proven spine. The three rules that keep it elegant: the mark never moves, holds last ≥ 4s, swaps take < 1s. CTA words render in gold so brand vs ask is instant.

THE STINGER (V6) = THE CTA MOMENT

The separate like-&-subscribe animation you described — bottom-right, pops in, does its thing, leaves. Because it's transient it can be louder and bigger than the watermark ever should be. It auto-keeps a schedule (calm / regular / often), never repeats the same type twice, and would suppress itself while alerts fire.

WHERE HANDLES GET READ

Physics: a 1080p frame on a 6.7″ phone is downscaled ~7×, so a 25px watermark word arrives at ~3.5px. Nobody reads handles there — they recognise them. Reading-size handles belong to the merged ticker pill you specced for Studio ([smile logo | prices | social handles], full-width — recorded, that's the next overlay round) and to the off-air card.

  • Sizing law: the face mark carries recognition — size the mark, not the text. Desktop audience → L. Phone-heavy audience → XL. Never below M. The phone frames in the panel are the judge.
  • Location law: top-left is the default — first thing the eye lands on, zero collisions (cams and alert toasts live bottom-right, clock chips top-right, lower-thirds own bottom-left). Switch to top-right only when a webcam runs bottom-right fullscreen. Bottom-right is the stinger's home — if the watermark ever sits there, the stinger politely stacks above it.
  • Shape law: pill — per the Round-4 verdict, the stadium is the circle's natural extrusion. V4 (bare) and V7 (outline) exist so you can see the pill earn its keep against bright and busy content.
  • The recommendation: V2 morph, top-left, size L + V6 stingers, bottom-right, regular + your merged ticker pill when overlays come back from pause. That's all three jobs, composed.
  • Cinema: scrapped, as ordered — delisted from the tournament. Its only survivor is this lab: the watermark it carried, now designed properly on its own.
