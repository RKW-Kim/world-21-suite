Round 7 — what's in the suite, and what's still missing

The material recipe that got us here (answering "what helped reach the obsidian variant the most?"): real displacement refraction under the glass (the nikdelvin port — edges genuinely lens the scene), layered translucent blacks instead of solid black, a top-lit tint gradient with a dark seam under the light, three-tier shadow discipline, and ONE platform accent per card in the bottom rim. Round 7 matte-ifies it: the crisp specular hairline is gone (diffuse top-light instead), a mineral grain layer adds tooth, and the chameleon variant adds the smart-material behaviour you described — hue, chroma and breath driven by the backdrop.

In the suite already: FOLLOW · LIKE · HEART · SHARE · BELL · TIP (M-PESA) · SUBSCRIBE (YouTube/Twitch/Kick) · SUPER THANKS · SUPER CHAT · MEMBERSHIP · CHEER (bits) · GIFTED SUBS · RAID · HYPE TRAIN · CLIP · TikTok GIFT · REACTION (like→love→wow) · STARS. Every platform tab shows its own set; nothing was left behind.

Honestly still missing (rare or platform-locked — say the word and they get built): TikTok gift tiers (Galaxy/Lion/Universe spectacles), Facebook wow/haha as separate reaction cards, Instagram badges, X tip jar, YouTube Super Sticker, Twitch hype chat, first-time chatter welcome, shout-outs / raids-out ("go follow them"), sub-iversary / milestone cards, and a top-donor king-of-the-hill board. Also worth saying: OBS browser sources can't see the video behind them, so in production the chameleon ships as ?tint=h,s (manual) or event-driven tints — in this lab it samples for real.

The logo verdicts, so you can overrule them: THE DRAW (smile wipes on as follow/subscribe lands) and THE NOD (grateful bow on tips) integrate; THE GLOWLINE (gold traces the smile on paid events) is paid-only; THE BLINK and THE WATCH were killed with probe evidence — "flicker, not charm".
