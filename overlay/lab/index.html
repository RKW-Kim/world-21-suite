SHAPE LANGUAGE — THE ANSWER YOU NEVER GOT

Verdict: the pill (stadium) is the right container — but the real soulmate is the arc. Your logo is two shapes: a circle and a smile-arc. Shapes complement it when they keep its continuous curvature: ① Pills for all chrome (ticker, alerts, chips, cams' name tags) — a pill is a stretched circle, so it echoes the face without competing with it; ② Squircles (22–30px continuous radii) for content cards — soft enough to belong, rigid enough to hold captures; ③ The arc itself as a motif — progress rings, underlines, the ghost smile watermark on charts (DESK's countdown ring is a literal smile-arc); ④ Circles for status dots and avatars. Avoid triangles and hexagons entirely — sharp corners fight the face. So: pill for chrome, squircle for cards, arc for accents — that's the whole system, and it's why Island R4 feels "right" to you.
