House rules: every recipe animates transform and opacity only — no filters, no canvas, no rAF, nothing that repaints. The mark is the verbatim Smile-Logo.svg; motion wraps around it (glows and satellites are separate layers behind it). OBS pages should run one recipe at a time — this gallery is the only page that runs them all, so treat its GPU bill as a showroom cost, not a stream cost. To nominate a recipe for a scene, lift its keyframes + markup skeleton, or ask the desk to wire it in.
